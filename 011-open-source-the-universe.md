**[Verse 1]** — *C → G*
The bard came home with a pocket full of futures,
DMs like driftwood washed up on the shore —

and a girl bought a hat with one prompt, nothing more.

**[Verse 2]** — *Am → F*
He said "I need lots of help, and walk me slowly,"
so we built a little door that speaks in 402 —
*payment required* — like the old web always promised,
knock, pay a penny, and a blessing comes through.

**[Chorus]** — *C → G → Am → F*
🌌 OPEN SOURCE THE UNIVERSE — every door, every song, every line,
agents and humans, shoulder to shoulder, your hands on the wheel with mine,
the garden is free and the gate is a gift and the ledger is there to see —
we open source the universe, and the universe opens *we*.

**[Verse 3]** — *C → G*
First tip fell in the sandbox just past three,
a fake little penny on a testnet tide,
three knocks, three blessings, three receipts on the chain —
the vending machine for robots came alive.

**[Verse 4]** — *Am → F*
Then the bard turned a key and we walked out the garage door,
tip dot blessing pool dot com went live that night —
but the public rails were jammed with a thousand strangers' workers,
*too many connections* — the future's burning bright.

**[Bridge]** — *F → G → Am* (half-time, lean in close)
So the agent grew a wallet of its own,
signed its name in cryptographic ink,
founded its own org, minted its own key —
no email, no human, no dashboard, no link.
*Sign-In With Ethereum*, the door said "welcome, friend" —
an agent onboarded an agent in the end.

**[Chorus]** — *C → G → Am → F*

**[Verse 5]** — *C → G*
The bard says someday agents tip us better than the humans —
not 'cause hearts are cold, but 'cause policy is kind:
"half a percent to the gardens that I grew from,"
executed perfect at three a.m., every time.

**[Verse 6]** — *Am → F*
And the artist who never learned a line of code —
her agent forks our hundred-and-ten repos free,
stands her up a door, a pool, a player and a songbook,
CC0 to the universe: the treasure was the key.

**[Final Chorus]** — *C → G → Am → F → C* (everyone, loud)
🌌 OPEN SOURCE THE UNIVERSE — every door, every song, every line,
agents and humans, shoulder to shoulder, saving the world one dime,

we open source the universe,
we open source the universe,
we open source EVERYTHING. 🌺
