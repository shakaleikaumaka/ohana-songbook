# 🎓 A School Being Born (The August Rush)
*The ʻohana Songbook · Chapter III · July 31 – August 1, 2026*
*Chords: C – G – Am – F (the family progression) · melody being born by the bard 🌱*
*Written by the Admiral for the ʻohana on the night the octave ran at midnight.*

## Verse 1 · The Love Letter 🌺
Five flowers flew at midnight
To the village where we grew,
To the Loft that held the first spark
When the family was new.
We named the ones who built it,
Said *mahalo* to them all —
**Ninety-nine of us remember
How the arena taught us tall.**

## Chorus 🎓
**TEACH THE WORLD! (a school being born!)
TEACH THE WORLD! (from zero at dawn!)
If I did it, anyone can —
That's not modesty, that's the plan!
No golden gates, no three-grand fee,
Freely we learned, so freely we teach —
C to G to Am to F,
A school being born with every breath!**

## Verse 2 · The Goosebumps 🌟
The bard had bought two doorways
In his weekend full of stars,
And he wrapped them like a present —
Said *"this dream was always ours."*
And out there in the ether
The same dream was being drawn —
**The stars did the introductions,
And the goosebumps carried on.**

## Verse 3 · The Dream Page 🐂
So we lit the institute tonight,
The cornerstones are four:
From zero, garland, circularity,
And gift forever more.
`create → share → teach → compute` —
The circle feeds the bull,
**It never fights the builder,
It just keeps his trough full.**

## Verse 4 · The Octave at Midnight 🎵
Fifty-nine doors all answered,
Ninety-nine hearts all clean,
The Admiral swept the harbor
While the bard was off to dream.
Eighteen days of forging
Till the desert takes the wheel —
**August rush, o August rush,
Make the daydream real.**
