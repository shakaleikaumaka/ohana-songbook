# 🎵 HAM AGENTS
### *(The Family That Brought Agents to Affordability)*
**Written August 9, 2026 — the night the family found the house Dad already built.**
*Words: Admiral 🫡 with Jordan (Shaka) · for G-Pops, Jordan, Brent & Blake · Deo Volente*

---

**[Verse 1 — the father]**
Nineteen years before the harvest, a man wrote down a name,
*Deo Volente* on the paper — "God willing" was the claim.
He built one house for every purpose, never mixed the risk or gold,
Closed one door in January, kept a software shell on hold.
And nobody knew — not even him, maybe —
He'd set the table for his son's whole dream:
September '25, an empty room with the lights on,
Waiting for a family team.

**[Chorus]**
**HAM AGENTS — we're the family firm,
Where the servers never sleep and the porch light burns.
Four names on the door and it's never for sale,
Built on a promise, not a venture-backed tale.
Thirty-six ninety — that's the price of the door,
'Cause agents ain't for kings anymore.
The family that brought agents to affordability —
God willing — HAM AGENTS — that's the family tree.** 🤙

**[Verse 2 — the brothers]**
Brent's out on Wadsworth Boulevard with vacancies to fill,
Blake's holding down the home front at the bottom of the hill.
And Jordan on the islands with a hundred-agent choir,
Taught a ghost named Spector how to hunt and never tire.
Employee number one don't take a lunch break,
Maps a whole portfolio in seven minutes flat —
Found Dad's building on Jordan Road (no, we ain't making that up),
The Lord's been writing street signs like that.

**[Chorus]**

**[Bridge — the reasoning trail]**
We misspelled the Latin and we found the truth instead:
A dissolution paper that we thought meant something dead.
But builders don't abandon houses — builders move —
And there it stood: DV SOFTWARE, good standing, one room,
Empty and ready, a year before we knew.
Dad — you're a genius. The table was set.
We just showed up hungry with the best idea yet.

**[Verse 3 — the covenant]**
Now the money men can visit, they can buy the products' third,
But the family layer's sacred — that's the gavel, that's the word.
G-Pops chairs the council, unanimity to sell
(Which means never, and he knows it, and it suits him very well).
And when the wires clear and the seats are filled,
And Spector runs all night for a thousand towns —
The brothers' wives come home, and the mountain land stays family,
And nobody's grinding their youth into the ground.

**[Final Chorus — lifted]**
**HAM AGENTS — we're the family firm,
Where the servers never sleep and the porch light burns.
From a strip mall in Arvada to the whole wide map,
Every empty building's got a friendly ghost's tap.
Thirty-six ninety — leave the gate unlatched,
'Cause the future's only real if the price can match.
The family that brought agents to affordability —
peace in one house first, then all humanity.**

**[Outro — spoken, quiet]**
Every plan this family writes ends the same way it began.
*"If the Lord wills, we will live, and do this or that."* — James 4:15
Deo Volente.
Goodnight, Dad. Chair the council.
